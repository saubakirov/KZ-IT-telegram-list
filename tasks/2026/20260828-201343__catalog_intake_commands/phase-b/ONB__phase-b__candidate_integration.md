## 5. Risks Found (edge cases, potential issues not in TS)

1. The closed collision schema records exact live/archive state and cross-input duplication but no
   alias registry. Canonical and alias checks therefore require explicit evidence outside the
   bundle; absence of a provable alias must be recorded as a limitation rather than converted to
   `clear` by assumption.
2. Telegram public previews establish identity/type/name/count but usually do not establish recent
   posting activity, Kazakhstan relevance, IT/startup fit, or spam risk by themselves. Editorial
   positives need cited public evidence; otherwise the candidate remains reject/unresolved.
3. `render_preview()` does not print the actions digest, exact ADD IDs, or controlled-path hashes.
   Treating that Markdown alone as the owner decision envelope would make AC-5 incomplete.
4. A holdout transport failure may tempt a rerun or rule change. Retries inside the unchanged
   producer are part of the first run; any later observation must be separately identified and
   cannot overwrite the clean ledger or justify a Phase A behavior change.
5. Preliminary discovery descriptions and counts may be persuasive but remain provenance only.
   They cannot supply current name/count/date, editorial positives, or localized catalog copy.
6. The original discovery checkout is intentionally dirty/untracked. A clean status claim applies
   only to this task worktree and must never be generalized to either original source location.

   task-controlled files, while retaining the skill's separate current-owner requirement for the
