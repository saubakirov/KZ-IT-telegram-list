## Research Plan

### Gather

- Decompose each record across five candidate dimensions: requested-handle binding, observed peer kind, continuity with the historical catalog row, living-replacement evidence, and independent death/closure evidence.
- Collect current target-specific Telegram preview facts for `datanomika` and `kzquake`, then seek dated independent listings, posts, or archives that bind the same handle/name/topic over time.
- Search public web indexes, community directories, repository history, and accessible web archives for `mobile_developers_kz` and `kzqacommunity`; reject generic contact shells, vacancy mentions, and unbound name matches.
- Record every source with observation/access date, URL or repository artifact reference, exact target binding, and limitations; actively seek counter-evidence to continuity and to death.

### Extract

- Cross-reference the five dimensions into a per-entry configuration space whose outcomes are `reclassification`, `link repair`, `archive`, or `unresolved`.
- Compare current evidence with the Phase D full-sweep, exact retry, and browser bundle; separate public target binding from historical community continuity and from member-count availability.
- Use at least one external source during extraction to test Telegram peer-kind semantics or a newly discovered replacement/closure claim rather than relying only on repository interpretation.
- State the minimum evidence package for each proposed mutation and identify which facts remain owner decisions rather than research conclusions.

### Challenge

- Test whether `groups → channels` for `datanomika` and `bots → channels` for `kzquake` could conceal username reassignment, product replacement, or a distinct peer despite matching visible names.
- Test every claimed replacement/death for `mobile_developers_kz` and `kzqacommunity` against same-community identity, date, peer kind, and independent-source requirements; a vacancy or shell alone must fail.
- Use external counter-searches for surviving communities, renamed handles, closure notices, and historical captures; eliminate configurations with unsupported continuity or death.
- Produce exact per-entry supportability and residual approval needs, including an explicit `unresolved` result when no safe mutation survives.
